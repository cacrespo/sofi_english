The Magic "-s" Rule with the Toons! (Unit 4)

He / She / It ➔ adds -s!
1. Dandy (walk / walks) to school every morning at 8:00 AM.
2. Poppy and Boxten (play / plays) in the schoolyard.
3. The English teacher (write / writes) new words on the board.
4. Sofía and her sister (eat / eats) breakfast before school.

don't / doesn't & Do / Does

a. Timmy ______ (don't / doesn't) drink cold soda at night.
b. The Toons ______ (don't / doesn't) go to school on Sundays.
c. ______ your brother ride a bike in the park? — Yes, he does!
d. ______ you like playing in Dandy's World, Sofía? — Yes, I do!

There is vs. There are in Dandy's Garden

There is (1) | There are (2 or more)
1. ______ a golden sun shining brightly in the blue sky.
2. ______ five tall trees in the peaceful nature park.
3. ______ twelve colourful butterflies flying near Flutter.
4. ______ a cute pet rock dog named Pebble resting on the grass.

Where is Pebble? (Prepositions of Place)

📥 IN (adentro) 🔛 ON (sobre) 👇 UNDER (debajo) 🙈 BEHIND (detrás) 👉 NEXT TO (al lado) ↔️ BETWEEN (entre)
a. Pebble is hiding ______ the big tree trunk.
b. The fish is swimming ______ the clear river water.
c. Pebble is sleeping ______ the wooden desk.
d. Flutter is flying ______ two pink flowers.
